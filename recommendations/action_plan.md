# Strategic Roadmap: Closing the Prosthetic Care Access Gap

## 1. Executive Summary

To overcome the twin challenges of **high clinical trial centralization** and **rural prosthetic provider deserts**, we propose an actionable three-pillar implementation strategy:
1. **Pillar I: Mobile Prosthetic Care & Rapid Fabrication Units (MPUs)**
2. **Pillar II: Tele-Orthotics/Prosthetics & Digital Socket Measurement**
3. **Pillar III: Legislative Parity & Rural Provider Incentives**

---

## 2. Pillar I: Mobile Prosthetic Units (MPUs)

### The Concept:
Rather than requiring rural patients to travel 100–230 miles round-trip for socket adjustments and gait alignments, specialized mobile prosthetic care clinics (built on heavy-duty commercial van or RV chassis) can rotate on bi-weekly schedules throughout rural West Virginia, Eastern Kentucky, and the Mississippi Delta.

### On-Board Capabilities:
* **Optical 3D Surface Scanners:** High-resolution handheld white-light/structured-light scanners to capture residual limb topography without messy plaster casting.
* **Rapid Direct Fabrication:** Desktop CNC socket carvers or large-format additive manufacturing (3D printing) using high-strength carbon-reinforced filaments (PETG/Carbon Fiber or Polypropylene).
* **Modular Alignment Jig & Dynamic Pressure Mapping:** Wireless in-socket pressure sensor sleeves to quantitatively verify load bearing and pressure distribution during ambulation.
* **Component Inventory:** Replacement liners, suspension sleeves, valves, feet, and adapters for immediate, same-day repairs.

### Hub-and-Spoke Operational Model:
```
             [Tertiary Academic Center / Central Fab Lab]
                   (Morgantown / Lexington / Jackson)
                                  │
                                  ▼
           [Regional Mobile Van Dispatch & Supply Base]

[Rural Clinic Hub A]     [Rural Clinic Hub B]     [Rural Clinic Hub C]
```

## 3. Pillar II: Tele-Prosthetics & Remote Fit Sensing

### High-Resolution Virtual Clinics:
* **Collaborative Care Encounters:** Prosthetic follow-ups conducted via telehealth connecting the patient and a local home health physical therapist with a certified prosthetist (CPO) located at the medical center.
* **Smart Liner / Dynamic Fit Sensors:** Deploying emerging sensorized sockets (such as those tested in University of Washington trial NCT07491614) that continuously log pressure, temperature, and volume fluctuations.
* **Prosthetic App & AI Fit Advisor:** A patient smartphone app allowing users to capture photos of red marks on their residual limb after doffing. Computer vision models classify pressure erythema vs. deep tissue strain, alerting the prosthetist when an immediate socket relief is necessary before an open wound occurs.

## 4. Pillar III: Legislative Parity & Workforce Investments

### 1. Interstate Medicaid Reciprocity for O&P Services:
* **Problem:** Delta patients in Mississippi living 75 miles from Memphis cannot easily utilize Tennessee providers due to out-of-state Medicaid authorization barriers.
* **Solution:** Establish statutory multi-state O&P compacts that allow automatic Medicaid enrollment reciprocity for border-adjacent medical referral regions.

### 2. Loan Forgiveness & Rural O&P Residency Grants:
* Allocate federal HRSA (Health Resources and Services Administration) grants to fund O&P resident positions dedicated to rural Appalachian and Delta community health centers.
* Provide student loan forgiveness for certified prosthetist-orthotists who commit to practicing in designated rural Health Professional Shortage Areas (HPSAs) for a minimum of 3 years.

### 3. Open-Source Hardware & Decentralized Prosthetics:
* Support open-source hardware repositories (analogous to open-source software) containing validated CAD/CAM prosthetic socket files and components compatible with global lower-cost manufacturing standards.
* Encourage clinical trial sponsors to include rural community cohorts in feasibility and pivotal trials, ending the exclusive monopoly of metropolitan research centers.

## 5. Implementation Roadmap (3-Year Horizon)

| Phase | Milestone | Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Year 1: Pilot** | Launch 1 Prototype MPU in Eastern Kentucky | Partner with UK HealthCare to deploy mobile clinic serving Perry, Letcher, and Pike counties; evaluate 6-month patient satisfaction and travel cost reduction. |
| **Year 2: Expansion** | Deploy Tele-Prosthetic Network in WV & MS | Integrate digital 3D scanners into 10 Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs) across rural West Virginia and the Mississippi Delta. |
| **Year 3: Policy** | Federal & State Parity Advocacy | Introduce rural O&P Medicaid reciprocity legislation and establish a national Open Prosthetic Atlas Registry for longitudinal outcome tracking. |
